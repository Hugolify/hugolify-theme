{{/* A frame of a video Uploadcare hosts, see func/GetVideoThumbnail.html, drawn on the fly by videoframe: the middle one, as Cloudinary picks it, or the one at 1s when the duration is unknown — a time past the end is an error. Drawn at the size of the video, it is resized as any image of the JSON-LD, see func/GetSchemaImage.html. Params: {map} url (string) the absolute URL of the video seconds (int) optional, its duration, 0 when unknown Returns: string, the URL of the frame — "" for a video Uploadcare does not host. */}} {{- $frame := "" -}} {{- with findRE `^https://[^/]*(ucarecdn\.com|ucarecd\.net)/[0-9a-f-]{36}/` .url 1 -}} {{- $seconds := $.seconds | default 0 -}} {{- $frame = (partial "func/GetSchemaImage.html" (printf "%svideoframe/%ds/" (index . 0) (cond (gt $seconds 0) (div $seconds 2) 1))).url -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $frame -}}
