{{/* GetTermPages The pages of a term, each listed once. On a nested taxonomy, Hugo also lists in a parent term the pages of its child terms, once per child term: a page tagged "parent", "parent/a" and "parent/b" shows up three times, and a page tagged "parent/a" only shows up too. Only the pages that carry the term itself are kept. Params: (page) the term Returns: the pages, in the order of term.Pages */}} {{- $term := . -}} {{- $pages := slice -}} {{- range uniq .Pages -}} {{- $page := . -}} {{- range .GetTerms $term.Data.Plural -}} {{- if eq .Path $term.Path -}} {{- $pages = $pages | append $page -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $pages -}}
