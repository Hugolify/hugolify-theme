{{/* Indexes the pages of a section by every key a taxonomy term may carry. A term filled from a section only holds a key, and that key takes one of two shapes: the folder name, or the anchorized title of the page in the primary language. The two differ when a CMS collapses the repeated separators of a title like "Penal law - Compliance", so both are indexed. Both are language independent, which is the point: indexing the title of the *current* language only resolves a page for as long as its title is left untranslated, and silently stops the day it is. Meant to be called through partialCached, the section being the cache key. - . (string) the section Returns a map, key to the page in the current language. */}} {{- $section := . -}} {{- $pages := dict -}} {{- range where site.Pages "Section" $section -}} {{- if ne .Path (printf "/%s" $section) -}} {{- $page := . -}} {{- $keys := slice (path.Base .Path) -}} {{- with index .AllTranslations 0 -}} {{- $keys = $keys | append (.Title | anchorize) -}} {{- end -}} {{- range $keys -}} {{- $pages = merge $pages (dict . $page) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $pages -}}
