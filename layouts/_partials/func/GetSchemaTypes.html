{{/* GetSchemaTypes The schema.org types a page or the site can declare, split by branch. Canonical list, shared with hugolify-admin so the select and the template that reads it cannot drift apart. The branch decides the role, not just the type: a place typed as an organization is an office someone works for, a place typed as a location is somewhere they work at. LocalBusiness belongs to both branches in schema.org — Thing > Organization > LocalBusiness and Thing > Place > LocalBusiness. It is listed as an organization here, because a business is something one works for, and so are its subtypes. The organizations descending from LocalBusiness are places as well, and only they take the properties of a place — geo above all: an Organization or a Corporation has an address, not coordinates. A page of any section can also be an article, by its schema.type — set for a whole section by a cascade — see pages/schema-single.html. Those types are no choice for a place, and stay out of `all`. The lists below stay generic. The types of a trade — a law firm, a clinic, a plumber — come from the theme of that trade (Lawyerify, Medicalify, Handify…), through an optional hook, func/GetSchemaTypesTheme.html. It returns a map with any of the keys below, each one a slice of types appended to the generic list of the same key; `all` is computed here. A LocalBusiness subtype goes under both `organization` and `local_business`: {{- $localBusiness := slice "HomeAndConstructionBusiness" "Plumber" -}} {{- return (dict "organization" $localBusiness "local_business" $localBusiness ) -}} Returns: map organization (slice of string) place (slice of string) local_business (slice of string) the organizations that are places too article (slice of string) the types of an article all (slice of string) organization and place, in select order */}} {{- $types := dict "organization" (slice "Organization" "Corporation" "LocalBusiness" "ProfessionalService" "EducationalOrganization" "NGO" ) "place" (slice "Place" "LandmarksOrHistoricalBuildings" "TouristAttraction" "CivicStructure" "Accommodation" "Residence" ) "local_business" (slice "LocalBusiness" "ProfessionalService" ) "article" (slice "Article" "BlogPosting" "NewsArticle" "TechArticle" "ScholarlyArticle" ) -}} {{- /* The types of the trade, from its theme. A key this partial does not return is ignored. */ -}} {{- if templates.Exists "_partials/func/GetSchemaTypesTheme.html" -}} {{- range $key, $more := partial "func/GetSchemaTypesTheme.html" -}} {{- with index $types $key -}} {{- $types = merge $types (dict $key (. | append $more | uniq)) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return (merge $types (dict "all" ($types.organization | append $types.place) )) -}}
