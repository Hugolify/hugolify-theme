{{/* GetSchemaTypes The schema.org types a page or the site can declare, split by branch. Canonical list, shared with hugolify-admin so the select and the template that reads it cannot drift apart. The branch decides the role, not just the type: a place typed as an organization is an office someone works for, a place typed as a location is somewhere they work at. LocalBusiness belongs to both branches in schema.org — Thing > Organization > LocalBusiness and Thing > Place > LocalBusiness. It is listed as an organization here, because a business is something one works for. Returns: map organization (slice of string) place (slice of string) all (slice of string) both, in select order */}} {{- $organization := slice "Organization" "Corporation" "LocalBusiness" "ProfessionalService" "LegalService" "MedicalOrganization" "EducationalOrganization" "NGO" -}} {{- $place := slice "Place" "LandmarksOrHistoricalBuildings" "TouristAttraction" "CivicStructure" "Accommodation" "Residence" -}} {{- return (dict "organization" $organization "place" $place "all" ($organization | append $place) ) -}}
