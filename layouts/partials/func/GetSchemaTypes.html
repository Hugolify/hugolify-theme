{{/* GetSchemaTypes The schema.org types a page or the site can declare, split by branch. Canonical list, shared with hugolify-admin so the select and the template that reads it cannot drift apart. The branch decides the role, not just the type: a place typed as an organization is an office someone works for, a place typed as a location is somewhere they work at. LocalBusiness belongs to both branches in schema.org — Thing > Organization > LocalBusiness and Thing > Place > LocalBusiness. It is listed as an organization here, because a business is something one works for, and so are its subtypes. The organization branch covers the trades the Hugolify flavours are built for: a law firm or a notary (Lawyerify), a clinic (Medicalify), a real estate agency (Realestatify). The organizations descending from LocalBusiness are places as well, and only they take the properties of a place — geo above all: an Organization or a Corporation has an address, not coordinates. A page of any section can also be an article, by its schema.type — set for a whole section by a cascade — see pages/schema-single.html. Those types are no choice for a place, and stay out of `all`. Returns: map organization (slice of string) place (slice of string) local_business (slice of string) the organizations that are places too article (slice of string) the types of an article all (slice of string) organization and place, in select order */}} {{- $organization := slice "Organization" "Corporation" "LocalBusiness" "ProfessionalService" "LegalService" "Notary" "MedicalOrganization" "MedicalClinic" "RealEstateAgent" "EducationalOrganization" "NGO" -}} {{- $place := slice "Place" "LandmarksOrHistoricalBuildings" "TouristAttraction" "CivicStructure" "Accommodation" "Residence" -}} {{- $localBusiness := slice "LocalBusiness" "ProfessionalService" "LegalService" "Notary" "MedicalClinic" "RealEstateAgent" -}} {{- $article := slice "Article" "BlogPosting" "NewsArticle" "TechArticle" "ScholarlyArticle" -}} {{- return (dict "organization" $organization "place" $place "local_business" $localBusiness "article" $article "all" ($organization | append $place) ) -}}
