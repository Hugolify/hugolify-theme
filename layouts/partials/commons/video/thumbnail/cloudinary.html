{{/* A frame of a video Cloudinary hosts, see func/GetVideoThumbnail.html: the URL of the video with .jpg for an extension, the middle frame. Params: {map} url (string) the absolute URL of the video Returns: string, the URL of the frame — "" for a video Cloudinary does not host. */}} {{- $frame := "" -}} {{- if findRE `^https://res\.cloudinary\.com/[^/]+/video/upload/` .url -}} {{- $frame = printf "%s.jpg" (replaceRE `\.[A-Za-z0-9]+$` "" .url) -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $frame -}}
