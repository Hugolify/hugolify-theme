{{/* /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt (https://llmstxt.org/). Each file is the body of its data file, data/llms.yml and data/llms-full.yml, written as is at the root of the site, where agents look for it whatever the language. A resource rather than an output format: the text is edited in the CMS and stands for no page of the site, so the site has nothing to turn on. Not translated, see func/GetUntranslatedData.html. No data file, or a body left empty, publishes no file. Called on the home page of each language, from _default/baseof.html: the same body gives the same resource, published once. */}} {{- range slice "llms" "llms-full" -}} {{- $file := . -}} {{- with (partialCached "func/GetUntranslatedData.html" $file $file).body | strings.TrimSpace -}} {{- (resources.FromString (printf "%s.txt" $file) (printf "%s\n" .)).Publish -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
